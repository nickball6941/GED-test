About the real tests

Montana accepts both the GED and the HiSET.

GED

  • 4 tests
  • Pass with 145 on each
  • Can be taken online at home with state approval

HiSET

  • 5 tests, scored 1 to 20
  • Need 8+ on each, 2+ on the essay, and 45 total
  • In person only: at-home HiSET ended in Montana Sept 1, 2025
